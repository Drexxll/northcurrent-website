Fair questions, straight answers

What does this cost me?

Nothing. No purchase, no lease, no fees. We own the machine, the inventory, and the risk. You own the location — that's your contribution, and it's why you get a cut of every sale.

I already have jukebox / gaming / ATM contracts.

Those contracts are almost always category-specific — music, gaming, cash access. Retail vending is its own lane and virtually never conflicts. We're happy to review your existing agreements with you before anything gets signed.

What if it doesn't work in my venue?

Our term is one year — not the three-year lock-in that's standard in this industry. If it isn't earning for both of us, you're not stuck with it.

Who deals with problems?

We do. One local operator — not a national call center. Machine issue, restock, product question: one call, handled.
